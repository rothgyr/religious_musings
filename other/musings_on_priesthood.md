# My Duties in my Priesthood
## Care for the Poor, the Sick, Afflicted, and the Lonely
1. Maintain a list of people in my **family**, **close friends/coworkers**, **neighborhood** and regularly inventory
  a. who needs assistance now, or is at high risk of needing help
  b. who is at risk and might be ready to be trained/assisted in a way that could reduce risks in the future
2. Pay a fast offering. Do more frequent, smaller fasts, with associated donations.
3. When fasting, add a phone call/text/visit/checkup of some kind to people on this list, even if it's just to say hello. Being present means they can trust you enough to tell you when emergencies arise. Fasting is a time to pay, and to minister, and visit the sick.
4. Check in with the Elders Quorum presidency and Relief Society President regularly to see if there's anyone I can help with
  - finding a job
  - giving rides
  - checking in on
  - managing billing
  - cleaning
  - watching children
  - delivering food
  - giving a blessing 

## Care for the Dead
1. Family history
2. Regular temple attendance *specifically to do work for the dead* and be beyond just being a temple worker
3. Make time for my wife (and children when it becomes applicable) to attend the temple

## Restoring Those Question Their Belonging
Who tends to question their belonging? Those who
  - feel that their values are threatened by policy
  - are victims of abuse
  - feel lonely
  - don't "qualify for the highest degree in the celestial glory" and have given up
  - associate church with "worthiness" or "judgement" or other "painful" situations
  - were wronged, and the wrong doer is at church - why is the victim ostricized when the wrongdoer who is "repentant" is welcomed with open arms?
  - have never *experienced* the power or love of God in their own lives, or perhaps have forgotten
  - are bad at chastity/word of wisdom/have a personalized look that doesn't conform to generic church looks
